## 🛡️ Cloudflare Outbound Encrypted Tunnel (Key Selling Point)

When users want to share their website or portfolio with clients or the world, traditional servers force them to open ports in their home router firewall. This exposes their home IP address, ISP, and physical location to automated port scanners and DDoS attacks.

**OmniHost Pro solves this permanently:**
- Reaches **outbound** to Cloudflare’s global edge network over an encrypted TLS tunnel.
- Cloudflare routes public visitors down through the existing outbound tunnel directly to the local machine.
- **Zero router configuration** and **zero firewall exceptions**.
- **Real Home IP Address is 100% Hidden**: Public visitors only see Cloudflare Anycast IP addresses.
