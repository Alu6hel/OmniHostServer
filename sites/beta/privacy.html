SECTION 01

The Local-First Core Doctrine

Alumungandr products, created and maintained by David Anthony Jones ("Alu"), operate on a strict Zero-Knowledge, Local-First paradigm. When you use our web utilities—such as the No-BS Invoice & Receipt Generator, the OmniHost Speed Test, or our downloadable APK applications—the processing happens on your hardware or within transient, non-persistent RAM buffers.

The Alumungandr Guarantee

We do not build advertising dossiers on you. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your IP address, device fingerprints, or business records under any circumstances.

SECTION 03

Speed Test & Diagnostic Telemetry

When executing an internet performance benchmark via our Speed Test utility:

  • The server streams pre-allocated random bytes from memory to measure throughput. No executable code is sent to your device.
  • Uploaded test chunks are discarded immediately upon reception—they are never written to physical disk.
  • If you choose to generate a public share link for your benchmark report, the public record displays an anonymized IP address (e.g. 192.168.*.*) to protect your location.
SECTION 04

Offline APK Application Integrity

All proprietary Android packages provided under the Alumungandr banner (including GalaxSee, SueChef, and OmniHost) are engineered for offline resilience:

They do not require online user authentication, do not connect to surveillance ad networks, and do not transmit contacts, device photos, or clipboard data to any external server.

SECTION 05

Zero Third-Party Data Sharing

We reject the surveillance capitalism model. We do not integrate:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics tracking beacons.
  • Facebook / Meta pixel tracking tags.
  • Cross-site behavioral data aggregators.

CDN and tunnel connectivity (such as Cloudflare Edge) are utilized strictly for encrypted transport and DDoS defense.

SECTION 06

Your Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

Regardless of whether you reside in the European Union, the United States, or anywhere globally, you enjoy the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You have the right to know what information is processed. Because our tools operate locally in RAM, there is typically zero personal record to inspect.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget Me): If you submit a support ticket or contact message, you may request its instant deletion at any time by transmitting an inquiry to our legal desk.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Every feature of our apps and utilities is equally available without requiring personal data surrender.
