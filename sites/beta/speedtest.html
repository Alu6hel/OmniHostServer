Understanding Your Real Broadband Metrics

The illusion of simplicity: your internet either works or it does not. But the exact second a video call lags or a web page takes an extra second to load, millions of people hit Google to diagnose their connection. OmniSpeed serves as the definitive diagnostic benchmark to hold internet service providers accountable, troubleshoot sluggish Wi-Fi routers, or decide whether your connection can handle remote production files.

What is Bufferbloat?
Bufferbloat occurs when network equipment queues excessive packets instead of transmitting them immediately. It causes latency spikes during heavy downloads or uploads, leading to sudden gaming disconnects and stuttering voice calls.
Why Multi vs. Single Stream?
Multi-connection testing utilizes multiple concurrent TCP sockets to maximize pipeline saturation across high-bandwidth lines, reflecting real-world downloads. Single-connection testing isolates individual socket constraints and single-server transfer speeds.
